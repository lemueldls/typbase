# Design notes

Typbase takes its product shape from Notion and Obsidian, its data model from Typst, and its editor from Mnemo. These are the other systems and talks that shaped it.

## Typst

[Typst](https://typst.app) gave the language and the idea that documents can be queried as data. A note is a Typst document, so prose, math, queries, exports, and plugin UI share one syntax.

## Notion and Obsidian

[Notion](https://www.notion.com) and [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md) shaped the product: daily notes, categories, backlinks, and files on disk.

## Mnemo

[Mnemo](https://github.com/lemueldls/mnemo) is the earlier Typst WYSIWYG editor. Its editor and engine were extracted into Typbase and published as `@typbase/codemirror` and `@typbase/engine`.

## The LEAST Private Operating System Ever Created

[The LEAST Private Operating System Ever Created](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M_720LesVg4) is the reference for reflective systems where everything is inspectable.

## Dynamic Documents as Personal Software

[Dynamic Documents as Personal Software](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MccJdr61xnc) is the reference for documents that run their own code.

## PlayBook: A Programmable Paper Notebook

[PlayBook: A Programmable Paper Notebook](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GurWDZ8ENpA) is the reference for programmable notebook pages.
